Frequently Asked Questions

What content is available on Cinematic?

Cinematic offers over 18,000 movies across all genres including Action, Comedy, Drama, Horror, Science Fiction, and many more. Our library is constantly updated with new releases, classics, and exclusive content.

How much does Cinematic cost?

We offer flexible subscription plans: Free Plan ($0/month with ads), Standard ($9.99/month for HD), Premium ($15.99/month for 4K), and Family Plan ($19.99/month for up to 6 profiles). All plans come with a 7-day free trial.

Can I download movies to watch offline?

Yes! Premium and Family plan members can download up to 100 movies to their devices for offline viewing. Downloads are available for 30 days or 48 hours after you start watching, whichever comes first.

How many devices can I use simultaneously?

The number of simultaneous streams depends on your plan: Free (1 device), Standard (2 devices), Premium (2 devices), and Family Plan (4 devices). You can create up to 6 user profiles on your account.

Can I cancel my subscription anytime?

Yes, you can cancel your subscription anytime without penalties or hidden fees. Your access will continue until the end of your current billing period. To cancel, go to Account Settings > Subscription > Manage Subscription.

What is Smart Discovery?

Smart Discovery is our AI-powered recommendation engine that learns from your viewing history and preferences to suggest movies you'll love. It analyzes millions of user behaviors to provide personalized recommendations tailored to your taste.

How does the Movie Request feature work?

Can't find a movie? Request it! Use our Movie Request feature to suggest titles you'd like to see on Cinematic. Our community votes on requests, and we prioritize the most popular ones when acquiring new content.

Is my personal data secure?

Absolutely. We use industry-standard encryption and security measures to protect your personal data. All communications are encrypted (SSL/TLS), and we never share your information with third parties without your consent. Review our Privacy Policy for more details.
